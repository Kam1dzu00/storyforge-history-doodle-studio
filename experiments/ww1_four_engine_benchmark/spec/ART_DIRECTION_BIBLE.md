# Creative production bible

- **Format:** premium 9:16 stylized WW1 historical editorial cartoon; never use chibi, anime, rubber-hose by default, emoji soldiers, trivial circles-and-rectangles, faceless stickers, or generic stock art.
- **Character:** ONE coherent exhausted British infantry-inspired WW1 figure (historical references must be checked), young adult, weathered face, readable eyebrows and eyes, olive puttees, period-appropriate helmet/uniform, dirt and fabric folds. Helmet is SEPARATE and catches naturally when rat startles him. Boots must have distinct contact shadows on boards/water. No insignia or caricature needed.
- **Design sheet:** turnaround frontal/3-quarter/profile, 8 expressions (neutral exhausted, concern, shiver, alarm, disgust, relieved half-smile, grief, resolve), 6 hand grips (open, mug, helmet, letter, rung, tension), 5 body key poses (crouch, duck, hop, climb, sit), 3 rat poses (compressed, stretched, running).
- **Colors:** #18232B ink shadows, #33454C night slate, #465747 coat, #8F8064 canvas, #685342 muddy wood, #B1A68B skin highlights, #C16C3A fire accent, #D0D5CC pale fog. These are palette intentions, not immutable keys.
- **Drawing:** intentional asymmetrical shapes, controlled variable-width outlines (rough target 5–9 px at final resolution for major silhouette, thinner interior lines), attractive face construction, layered fill shading. Avoid muddy flat low-contrast patches.
- **Atmosphere:** 3–5 depth planes (foreground rain, character, midground duckboards/sandbags, distant ruined horizon, background sky). Rain/drips loop with offsets; smoke deforms/drifts and reacts to blasts; pooled water uses constrained ripple animation. Never cover face with oversized FX.
- **Comedy:** rat jump-and-helmet-catch (~12.05–13.3), tiny food disgust beat (~18.6–20.6). Everything else sober and moving.
- **Continuity:** keep hat damage, face proportions, sleeve color, right/left side props, and screen direction consistent. Establish trench wall geography and preserve light direction. No gravity violations.
- **Text:** clean large karaoke captions (2–5 words displayed at once), off-white fill + dark outline/drop shadow, highlight single active spoken word; avoid face and UI obstruction; use real words-onsets after forced alignment. Final ending on-screen title matches spoken narration or is clearly separate.
- **Historical care:** represent trench life without implying every trench was uniformly ankle-deep or every soldier served multiple continuous years in trench; narration simplifies experiences but must not assert impossible universal conditions. Verify uniform and context with historical sources before publication.
- **Sound:** sober adult English narrator; use consistent performance, -16 LUFS approximate target for web stereo unless producer changes, true peak <= -1 dBTP, no overly loud shell blast. Document all sound/music rights. No copyrighted commercial songs without rights.
